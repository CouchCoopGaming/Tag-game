Expert chains use the locked motor. Coyote is 0.10 s, jump buffer is 0.16 s, and cling grace is 0.08 s. Nothing in these chains asks for a tighter window. Beginner time is the ground nav at sprint 13.8, the same grid the chase sim uses. A route is in band when the save is between -6% and 25%. The old 10–25% band was at sprint 12. Zip, climb, and wall-run did not speed up, so ground sprint closed that gap.
